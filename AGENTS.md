# cocoach

AI driving coach for iRacing.

## Product

- **Live coaching:** watches the driver's live iRacing session and coaches in real time on racing line, braking, and throttle.
- **Memory:** remembers each driver across sessions: recurring mistakes, goals, and progress.
- **Post-race chatbot:** after a race, the driver can chat with the coach about how it went and what to work on next.
